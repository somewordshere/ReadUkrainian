Static Ukrainian pronunciation audio
====================================

MP3 files in voice-specific subdirectories are generated at build time by
scripts/speech/generate-speech-assets.mjs with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech
(Chirp 3 HD voices). Each directory's manifest.json records the voice, audio
settings, source stories and the exact text sent for every word.

Words missing from these files are synthesized on demand by /api/speech with the
same voice and settings.
